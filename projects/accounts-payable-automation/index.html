Accounts Payable Automation

A structured workflow that moves invoices from email intake to reviewed accounting records while keeping exceptions and approvals visible.

Invoice workflowFrom inbox to reviewed record.
CaptureValidateReviewSync
The problem

Too many handoffs, without one reliable view.

Invoice processing was spread across emails, attachments, manual data entry, document folders, and approval steps. Missing details and duplicate invoices made the process harder to track and review consistently.

01 / CONTRIBUTION

Designed around review, not blind automation.

I built the workflow that extracts invoice details, validates the data, checks for duplicates, organizes documents, and keeps review and approval states visible.

02 / WORKFLOW

A clear path with visible exceptions.

  • Collect invoice emails and supported attachments.
  • Extract key invoice fields and validate them.
  • Flag duplicates, missing details, and unusual records for review.
  • Organize approved documents and pass approved records to QuickBooks.
03 / TECHNOLOGIES

Practical components, connected carefully.

Workflow automationEmail processingDocument extractionValidation rulesQuickBooks integrationData storage
04 / FUNCTIONALITY

One consistent operational process.

Invoices move through a defined workflow while exceptions, duplicate checks, approvals, and accounting handoff remain visible to the people responsible for reviewing them.
