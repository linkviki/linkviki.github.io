AI-Assisted Email Dashboard

A customized inbox workspace that helps teams review, classify, route, and act on incoming messages without hiding decisions from people.

Inbox intelligenceImportant messages, brought into focus.
CollectClassifyConfirmRoute
The problem

Important work was buried inside one busy inbox.

Operational, financial, customer, and informational emails all arrived together. Manual sorting took time and made it harder to identify messages that needed timely follow-up.

01 / CONTRIBUTION

Made AI suggestions visible and reviewable.

I designed the message intake and review experience, including configurable categories, priority suggestions, routing states, search, and a human confirmation step before downstream action.

02 / WORKFLOW

Assistance first, confirmation before action.

  • Collect messages and the metadata required for review.
  • Suggest a category, priority, and intended workflow.
  • Present the suggestion with the original message for human confirmation.
  • Route confirmed messages into focused queues and track follow-up status.
03 / TECHNOLOGIES

A focused combination of AI and workflow tools.

Email APIsAI classificationNatural language processingWeb dashboardSearch and filteringWorkflow automation
04 / FUNCTIONALITY

A clearer review process with people in control.

The dashboard creates focused queues and visible follow-up states while keeping classification and routing decisions reviewable by the person handling the inbox.
