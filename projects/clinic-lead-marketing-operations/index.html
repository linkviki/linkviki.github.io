The problem

Scattered conversations made ownership unclear.

Lead information and marketing activity can become spread across forms, inboxes, spreadsheets, and individual notes. That makes it difficult to understand where an enquiry came from, what should happen next, and which conversations still need attention.

01 / CONTRIBUTION

Designed a shared operational flow.

I designed the system around a shared lead record, clear stages, ownership, follow-up dates, and campaign-source visibility. The workflow keeps operational actions distinct from clinical records.

02 / WORKFLOW

From new enquiry to an accountable next action.

  • Collect enquiries from approved lead sources.
  • Create a shared record with source, owner, stage, and next action.
  • Move each conversation through follow-up and consultation stages.
  • Review campaign activity and outstanding team actions from one dashboard.
