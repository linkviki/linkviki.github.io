Hotel Reporting Dashboard

A reporting workflow that turns separate daily revenue and booking files into one clear management view.

Daily performanceValidated reports. One clear view.
RevenueOccupancyADRRevPAR
The problem

Separate reports made the full picture harder to see.

Revenue and booking reports arrived as different files with their own structures, dates, and totals. Combining them manually made daily checks and forward-looking reporting difficult.

01 / CONTRIBUTION

Built the reporting path from source to summary.

I built the process that extracts reports, checks the data, combines the results, calculates core hotel metrics, and prepares the dashboard and management summary.

02 / WORKFLOW

Validation before visualization.

  • Collect daily revenue and booking reports.
  • Check dates, expected fields, totals, and file completeness.
  • Combine validated records and calculate revenue, occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR.
  • Prepare the dashboard, management summary, and booking forecast.

A dependable reporting toolkit.

04 / FUNCTIONALITY

Daily performance with data issues surfaced early.

The reporting process produces a consistent view of performance and forward bookings while making data issues visible before the summary is shared.
